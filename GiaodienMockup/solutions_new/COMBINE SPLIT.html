Combine bills at POL,
split D/O at destination.

When one vessel carries containers for several customers, issuing one Master B/L instead of many — then splitting House B/Ls per customer — cuts line charges at origin and unlocks D/O margin at destination. Worked example below: 1 vessel, 5 containers, 3 customers.
