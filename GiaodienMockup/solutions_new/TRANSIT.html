Transit service via Vietnam to Laos / Cambodia / China; via Thailand to Cambodia / Myanmar

With over 3,000 km of coastline, Vietnam serves as a vital transit hub, helping neighboring countries such as Laos and Cambodia handle imports and exports through its seaports. Cross-border road access lets customers choose the route that best optimizes cost and logistics efficiency, based on the delivery location:

01
To Laos
Cargo for Vientiane typically transits through Hai Phong Port, Vietnam.
02
To Cambodia
Cargo for Svay Rieng / Phnom Penh moves via Cat Lai Port, Vietnam.
03
To China
For Yunnan or Guangxi, transiting through Hai Phong Port is a cost-effective option.
04
Via Thailand
Depending on route distance, transit via Thailand into Laos or Myanmar is also viable.
