Release Delivery Order (D/O) to consignee(s) at destination

As destination agent, SHOPTRANS represents the overseas origin agent to release Delivery Orders to the respective consignees for each nominated shipment — closing the final handover before cargo delivery.

01
D/O Release
Acting on behalf of the origin agent, SHOPTRANS issues the Delivery Order to each nominated consignee, matched per shipment.
02
Charges & Settlement
Coordinates directly with the shipping line to settle destination local charges or verify prepaid status, and handles collection & disbursement on the origin agent's instruction.
03
Value-Added Handling
Beyond D/O release — customs clearance, trucking, container unstuffing supervision and destination handling for smooth, efficient delivery.
