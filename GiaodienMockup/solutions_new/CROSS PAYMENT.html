Cross-country payment & collection

With an extensive network of branches worldwide, SHOPTRANS is a trusted partner for freight forwarding clients. Beyond logistics, we also facilitate efficient cross-border payment solutions — clients have two options:

01
Direct International Payment
Settle transactions directly from one country to another, paying the servicing branch wherever the work is performed.
02
Localized Payment via Branches
Pay service fees through a local SHOPTRANS branch in your own country and currency, without cross-border transfers.

For example, a company in China using transport services in Vietnam can settle fees in RMB or USD with SHOPTRANS China — no need to remit directly to SHOPTRANS Vietnam. Paying Vietnam directly remains an option if preferred.

Localized payment advantages
Reduced banking fees Minimized FX risk Streamlined documentation

This approach optimizes efficiency — especially for short-haul routes or urgent air shipments — letting clients simplify financial operations while enhancing transport performance. As a trusted partner, SHOPTRANS is dedicated to resolving your challenges effectively and efficiently.
