About Shoptrans

Shoptrans is a comprehensive end-to-end supply chain management system in logistics. Every step and process in the transportation of goods — from the point of origin to the final destination — is streamlined through an efficient execution platform. By eliminating traditional, manual, and fragmented logistics practices, Shoptrans introduces a standardized, unified, and fully integrated system.
